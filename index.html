The lamplighter

Every October, Old Wick walks Hollow Row with a candle burning on his head and a hook to light the lamps. He does not sell candles. He keeps one burning.

This year he hung his lantern on Solana. Every trade lets a drop of wax fall inside. Nine drops in ten stay in the lantern. One he keeps for the walk.

On the last night of October the candle gutters, the lantern cracks, and the wax runs out as SOL. First to the circle nearest the flame, then to the line of lanterns behind it, then to every window that kept a candle lit.

The counting

  • SnapshotTaken at a random moment between 28 Oct 00:00 and 31 Oct 00:00 UTC. The time is not announced in advance.
  • ProofThe snapshot time and the full holder list are published together with the payout, so anyone can check them on-chain.
  • ExcludedBonding curve and liquidity pools, exchange wallets, dev wallets, and every wallet that bought in the launch block.
  • Paid inSOL, from the lantern wallet, on 31 Oct by 20:00 UTC.

Burn calendar

  1. 17 OctThe lantern is lit. Contract and lantern wallet go public.
  2. 17–28 OctWax collects. The lantern balance is visible at all times.
  3. 28–31 OctThe snapshot window. The snapshot is taken at an unannounced moment in these 72 hours.
  4. 31 OctThe snapshot time, the full holder list and every share are published. The lantern opens.
  5. After HalloweenIf the volume holds, Old Wick lights it again for New Year.
